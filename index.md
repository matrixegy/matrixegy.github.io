The next-15-full-stack-repair-shop is built with Next.js 15. It offers features like type-safe forms and role-based authentication. You can track repairs, manage customers, and handle payments—all from one place.

- Easy management of repairs and customer information.

- A responsive design that works on various devices.
- Integration with popular databases like PostgreSQL.
